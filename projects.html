Selected academic work · 01

DATABASE DEVELOPMENT

Relational Database Design & Development

This area of coursework explores how information can be organised into related tables to support accurate storage, retrieval, and management of data.

What the project involves

  • Designing tables and defining suitable columns.
  • Identifying primary and foreign keys.
  • Establishing relationships between tables.
  • Using SQL DDL statements to create database objects.
  • Practising data insertion and retrieval queries.

Skills being developed

Relational thinking, SQL syntax, data integrity, attention to detail, and structured problem-solving.

My learning reflection

Database development is teaching me that a database is more than a collection of tables. Each relationship and constraint influences the consistency of the information stored. Understanding why an error occurs, rather than simply changing code until it works, is an important part of building my confidence.

Selected academic work · 02

BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Data Preparation & Power BI Visualisation

This area of study focuses on exploring datasets, preparing information for analysis, and presenting findings in visual formats that are easier to interpret.

What the project involves

  • Exploring the structure and quality of a dataset.
  • Using Power Query to prepare and transform data.
  • Examining relevant measures and patterns.
  • Creating appropriate charts and visualisations.
  • Organising visuals into a coherent dashboard.

Skills being developed

Data preparation, analytical reasoning, visual communication, interpretation, and dashboard design.

My learning reflection

Data visualisation involves more than making information look attractive. A useful chart must communicate the correct message, use appropriate comparisons, and avoid misleading interpretations. I am learning to consider both the quality of the underlying data and the needs of the people who will use the results.
