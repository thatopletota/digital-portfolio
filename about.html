HELLO, THERE!

I'm Thato Precious Letota.

I am a first-year Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student with an interest in learning, personal development and the relationship between data and decision-making.

I would describe myself as reserved, determined and charismatic. I appreciate the value of listening, observing and thinking things through, while remaining committed to the goals I set for myself.

The phrase Quiet Powerhouse captures the way I see my potential. I believe that ambition does not always need to announce itself. Sometimes, it is expressed through consistency, resilience, preparation and the willingness to keep learning.

This portfolio documents that process. It is a place to reflect on my academic experiences, demonstrate my developing abilities and recognise the lessons that contribute to my growth.

WHERE I'M HEADED

Curiosity with a purpose.

Business Intelligence and Data Analytics offers an opportunity to explore how information can be transformed into useful insights. I am interested in developing the technical knowledge and analytical judgement needed to understand information and communicate findings clearly.

As I progress through my studies, I hope to strengthen my problem-solving, communication, collaboration and digital skills. I also want to become more confident in applying classroom knowledge to practical situations.
